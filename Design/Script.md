## 0 — THE CHAIR

*Black. Rain. Under it, TV static, low and constant. Fade up on a security-camera angle — high corner of a small living room. Hold it for three seconds before cutting to a normal shot. The player will see this angle again.*

*ELIAS in an armchair, facing the TV. On the side table: a tipped prescription bottle, three pills in his palm, an alarm clock ticking, a folded paper. He tosses the pills back, swallows dry, coughs. Rain lashes the window. Wind rattles the glass. In the thunder, barely:*

**KIDNAPPER** *(a whisper inside the storm)*
This isn't a game.

*Elias's eyes go to the corners of the room. Nothing. He looks at the folded paper. Doesn't open it. Crumples it tighter and tosses it at the bin. It bounces off the rim.*

**ELIAS**
*Keep it together. You're better than this.*

*The phone rings. He watches it ring three times. Answers.*

**KIDNAPPER** *(phone; heavy processing; warm)*
Hey, champ. How's it going?

**ELIAS**
*Who is this?*

**KIDNAPPER**
Shh. Don't worry about that. Your son's safe with me.

**ELIAS**
*David's at a friend's. I dropped him—*

**KIDNAPPER**
You did. Did you look back?

*A beat. A gunshot on the line. A child screams. Then crying, far from the phone.*

**ELIAS**
*David— DAVID—*

**KIDNAPPER** *(brightening)*
Great. Now you're listening. You'll get a text with an address. Don't call anyone, Elias. If you do — *(softly, imitating)* — pow.

*Dial tone. Elias stands. Takes one step toward the door. The image tears — heartbeat, distortion — and drops to static.*

## 1 — THE FLOOR

*Gameplay. Back bedroom. Elias on the floor, on his side. The storm, muffled. Control returns as he pushes up.*

**ELIAS**
*The floor. Why am I on the floor.*

*Nightstand: a flashlight. Picking it up is the tutorial.*

*Bathroom. Mirror. The prescription bottle's twin — his name on the label. He shakes it.*

**ELIAS**
*Two left.*

*Kitchen. A grocery list on the fridge in his handwriting. The cellar door: he tries it.*

**ELIAS**
*Stuck. Always has been.*

*A single thud from below. He waits. Nothing.*

**ELIAS**
*Pipes.*

*Living room. TV static. The phone face-down on the carpet.*

**ELIAS**
*I dropped it.*

*The front door. He tries it.*

**ELIAS**
*Locked. From the outside.*

*On the side table — where the phone was — a folded note. He unfolds it. Handwriting.*

**NOTE**
The key is where you keep her ring.

*No subtitle. He crosses straight to a drawer without searching. A wedding ring. A key beside it. He takes the key. The ring stays. He does not look at it.*

---

## 2 — THE YARD

*Rain, live now, loud. The backyard. The main house fills the frame above the fence, dark, every window black.*

*The phone buzzes. A text. An address.*

**ELIAS**
*That's the house. That's… why would he bring him here?*

*A short scripted camera lift: the back of the main house, rain sheeting off the roof. Then control.*

*The path leads around the side. Where the side yard meets the driveway: two cars. His. And one he doesn't know, with a child's booster seat in the back. He walks past it. No line.*

---

## 3 — THE SIDE OF THE HOUSE

*Trees along the wall. A megaphone bolted under the eave crackles on.*

**KIDNAPPER** *(megaphone; heavy processing; far)*
Welcome! You made it. I know this is confusing, but bear with me — it'll all make sense. The side door's open. Well. It will be. Don't get scared, don't be afraid. After all, you're not alone.

*Notes pinned to trunks and fence posts. He unpins the first.*

**ELIAS**
*This looks familiar.*

*The image tears for one second — heartbeat — and settles.*

**ELIAS**
*I don't know from where.*

**KIDNAPPER**
Don't worry about it, Elias. You'll be okay.

*He looks up at the house.*

**ELIAS**
*It isn't how I remember it.*

**KIDNAPPER**
Memories change, don't they?

*The notes lead to the side door and its positional lock. The player solves it. The door opens. The megaphone clicks once more.*

**KIDNAPPER**
Good. Now — inside voices.

*It clicks off. It does not come on again.*

---

## 4 — THE HOUSE

*Dust. A clock ticking somewhere. The storm, faint through the walls. The front door, from inside: boards, nails.*

**ELIAS**
*Nailed from in here.*

*Newspapers on the table, the counters, the stairs. He reads.*

**ELIAS**
*"Family missing." "Last seen at a friend's house."*

*A second.*

**ELIAS**
*Same. Different year.*

*A third. Older. A rectangle cut from the middle of the column.*

**ELIAS**
*"—wife and son. Husband—"*

*The rest is gone. He puts this one down faster than the others.*

*The radio on the sideboard hisses on by itself.*

**KIDNAPPER** *(radio; medium processing)*
You're not alone in here, Elias.

*A thud from another room. He goes. Nothing.*

**ELIAS**
*Keep it together.*

**KIDNAPPER** *(radio)*
I've been with you the whole time.

*A phone rings in an empty room. He finds it. Answers.*

**KIDNAPPER** *(phone; medium)*
Just checking in. How are you holding up?

*Beat.*

**KIDNAPPER**
That's good. That's really good.

*Click.*

*Radio (batteries, then tuning), picture frames turned to a pattern, the keypad — their wedding date, though nothing says so. The house opens room by room.*

---

## 5 — DAVID'S ROOM

*Upstairs. A door with a name on it in wooden letters. The player opens it.*

*A boy's room, untouched, six years of dust. On the door frame: pencil marks and dates, climbing — and stopping. Nothing above the last one.*

*Elias walks through it. He says nothing. He does not look at the frame. This is the only room in the house where he has no line.*

*On the desk, a birthday card. He opens it.*

**ELIAS**
*"Happy ninth, champ."*

*The date inside is the safe code. He closes the card and sets it exactly where it was.*

---

## 6 — THE BASEMENT

*The basement door. Down. Red light. A deep hum under everything. Ten steps in, the flashlight flickers and dies.*

**ELIAS**
*Come on. Come on—*

**KIDNAPPER** *(no source; light processing; at his ear)*
Why do you need the light? You already know what's here.

*A cassette deck on a shelf clicks on by itself.*

**CHILD** *(tape; sobbing)*
Please… please, let me go.

*Click. A second voice.*

**CHILD** *(tape; barely there)*
I want to go home.

*Click. A gunshot. The scream. The same scream.*

**ELIAS**
*That was on the phone. That was— that was tonight—*

**KIDNAPPER**
You know exactly what this is.

*A table. A sheet over a small shape. The camera stays on Elias's hands as he lifts one corner. On his face. We never see what is under it.*

**ELIAS**
*It's not him.*

*He lets the sheet fall. He stands there too long.*

**KIDNAPPER** *(gently)*
No. Not him.

*Further in: bodies, adult. A door that opens onto one standing behind it — the scare. Notes on the walls in the handwriting the player now recognizes without being told.*

---

## 7 — THE ROOM OF MONITORS

*A door at the end of the red rooms. Inside: a desk, a wall of monitors. The side yard. The kitchen. David's room. The red rooms. And one labeled COTTAGE — LIVING RM: a high-corner shot of an armchair and a TV. The opening frame of the game.*

**ELIAS**
*That's my chair.*

*On the desk: a microphone. A cassette deck wired to a phone. A row of switches labeled in marker — MEGAPHONE, RADIO, HALL PHONE, BASEMENT LIGHTS. A plague-doctor mask, set down carefully. A pill bottle. He turns it. His name.*

**ELIAS**
*Mine.*

*A laminated card. Ticks in marker, every box.*

**CHECKLIST**
1. Relock side door ☑
2. Notes — yard ×6, house ×9 ☑
3. Rewind tapes ☑
4. Load call — number: *[his own number, handwritten]* ☑
5. Lock cottage ☑
6. Key → her drawer ☑

**ELIAS**
*That's my number.*

**KIDNAPPER** *(no processing now)*
Then who's been calling you, Elias?

*A monitor flicks to a replay, timestamped an hour ago. The kitchen upstairs. Elias, crouched inside a closet, door cracked, staring out at an empty room.*

**ELIAS**
*It was right there. It was— I saw it—*

**KIDNAPPER**
Was it?

*A drawer, half open. Wallets. A child's shoe. Another. He closes the drawer. Behind the desk, a hatch, and stairs going down.*

---

## 8 — THE TUNNEL

*Narrow. Long. Bare bulbs on a wire, every third one dead. His footsteps.*

**KIDNAPPER**
You've never been down here.

*It isn't a question.*

**ELIAS**
*No.*

**KIDNAPPER**
I have.

---

## 9 — THE DOOR

*The tunnel ends in a small concrete room. Stairs up on one side. On the other, a heavy door lined in foam. Behind it, a man's voice, ragged.*

**SURVIVOR** *(through the door)*
Hello? Is someone — please. Please, I can hear you. He's coming back.

**ELIAS**
*Hold on. Hold on, I'll—*

*The door doesn't move.*

**KIDNAPPER**
Not yet. Up first.

*The stairs. A door at the top. It opens into his kitchen.*

---

## 10 — THE CHAIR, AGAIN

*The cottage. The cellar door he couldn't open, open behind him. The kitchen. The grocery list. The living room. The chair. The TV is on — and it isn't static.*

*It's playing the call. Audio only, a waveform on the screen. And the voice saying the words is his. No processing. Just his voice.*

**TV**
Hey, champ. How's it going?

**ELIAS**
*No. No, no—*

**TV**
Shh. Don't worry about that. Your son's safe with me.

*He backs into the side table. The note. The alarm clock, still ticking.*

**KIDNAPPER** *(everywhere; it is the same voice as the TV)*
She was going to take him, Elias. What else could we do?

*The speaker tag on Elias's next subtitle is red. It stays red.*

**ELIAS**
*I didn't—*

**KIDNAPPER**
We did.

*A sound from below. A heavy door, opening.*

---

## 11 — THE CHAMBER

*Down. The foam door stands open. The chamber: a chair, a man tied to it, a drain in the floor. He lifts his head, sees Elias, and his face breaks open with relief.*

**SURVIVOR**
Oh God — oh, thank God — please, he's coming back, he'll — get me out. Get me out.

*Elias crosses to him. Kneels. His hands go to the ropes.*

**SURVIVOR**
Did he get you too? Your kid — he said he had a kid, he kept saying—

*Elias's hands stop.*

**ELIAS**
*I see now.*

*Then he laughs. One low laugh, then more. It is the first time in the game we have heard him. It is the Kidnapper's voice, unprocessed, and it has always been.*

*The survivor's face changes.*

**SURVIVOR**
No. No, that's — that's him. You're — you told me you had a son. You told me you had a *son*—

*A knife scrapes across the table as Elias picks it up. He stands. The camera goes to the drain.*

*Black. Silence.*

---

## 12 — AFTER

*Credits, over silence. Halfway through, faint under the black: rain.*

*After the last credit — the security-camera angle. The armchair. The TV, static. A phone rings.*

*Once. Twice. Three times.*

*Cut.*

# DIRECTOR POOL

*Lines the Kidnapper system fires on triggers between scripted scenes. Never repeat a line in one playthrough.*

**Act 1 — Yard (megaphone)**
- Take your time. He's not going anywhere.
- Left. No — your other left.
- You're doing so well.
- Cold out. You should've brought a coat.

**Act 2 — House (radio / TV / phone)**
- Every room, Elias. Don't skip any.
- You used to have a house like this. Didn't you.
- Read them. Read all of them.
- Almost. Almost.

**Act 3 — Basement (whisper)**
- Breathe.
- It's just dark. You've been in the dark before.
- Don't step there.
- I'm right here.

**Episode barks (apparition active)**
- Don't run. It's just us.
- Hide if you want. I know the house.
- He can't hurt you. He's not real. Neither am I.

**Blackout lines (on waking at checkpoint)**
- Welcome back.
- You were gone a while. I tidied up.
- Did you sleep? You looked like you needed it.
- I moved some things. You'll find them.

**Survivor — through the door (Scene 9, if the player lingers)**
- Are you still there? Don't leave. Don't leave.
- He talks about his kid. The whole time. The whole time.
- I have a daughter. Please. I have a daughter.

**Blackout world-changes** *(one per blackout, in order, per act)*
- Yard: a seventh note, on the cottage door.
- House: the radio on in a room it wasn't in; a door open that was locked; the newspapers restacked.
- Basement: a new tape queued on the deck; a body moved closer to the stairs.
